Reflux ground rules

  • Wake drink: 300–400 ml water + pinch of salt + 1 tbsp ACV. Sip upright 5–10 min. Wait 20–30 min before breakfast. Stop ACV if burn starts.
  • Stop eating 3 hrs before lying down. Dinner is 8:30 PM — then a 10-min upright walk.
  • Weigh raw grams for chicken, milk, muesli, whey, oil (level tsp). Eyeballing breaks the deficit.
  • Olive oil hard cap: 3 level tsp/day (this plate uses 1.5 tsp). Level tsp, not a free pour.
  • Goodlife toned milk + toned dahi for cooking. Snack buttermilk: 200 ml from the 1 L tetra — plain salted only, no masala/pudina/chili. Marinate chicken with toned dahi only (no packaged marinade). No coffee, alcohol, or soda. No tomato, raw onion, garlic, red chili, mint, lauki, cauliflower.
  • After lunch/dinner: saunf or ajwain water. Sit upright 20–30 min. Elevate bed head if nights are rough.
  • Week 1 scale may rise 1–1.5 kg (glycogen water). Judge the 7-day average only.
  • Soft weekends: keep the wake drink + breakfast bowl, plus one solid protein meal (chicken ≥150 g, 2–3 eggs, or paneer ≥100 g). Other meals flexible — avoid deep-fried and late heavy dinners.

Workout

How the week works

Every day: morning incline + core + Boldfit bands + pull-up ladder. Gym nights Mon / Tue / Thu / Fri (A / B / A / B). Miss the gym? Use the Home A / Home B block on that day’s tab (bands + pull bar + skip rope). Wed + Sat already home-only. Sun = morning + meal prep.

  • Morning cardio + core + bands first. Evening lift on gym days (or Home A/B if you skip). Stay upright where you can.
  • Train at least 2 hours after lunch. Never right after a meal. Dinner stays at 8:30 after the session.
  • Bicycle crunch is allowed (food manages GERD). Keep the low back pressed to the floor. Stop if lumbar pain — swap to dead bug / bird dog / side plank that day.
  • Inverted row (lying pull under a bar/table, knees bent OK) builds toward pull-ups. Advance: hang → inverted row 3×10 → scapular pulls → Boldfit band-assisted pull-ups → slow negatives → strict pull-ups.
  • Skip rope: soft knees, easy pace. Back or reflux flare → swap to incline walk for that cardio slot.
  • Avoid heavy barbell deadlifts, bent-over barbell rows, and good mornings while the back settles.
  • Avoid deep forward folds or inversions on a full stomach.
  • Sip water through the session; don't gulp large amounts at once.
  • Protein ~150 g/day from the weekday plate (whey is in breakfast). Add-ons only if a meal is missed or the session was brutal.
