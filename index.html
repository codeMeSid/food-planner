Reflux ground rules

  • Stop eating 3 hrs before lying down. Dinner is 8:30 PM — then a 10-min upright walk.
  • Weigh raw grams for chicken, dal, paneer, soya, oil (level tsp). Eyeballing breaks the deficit.
  • Olive oil hard cap: 3 level tsp/day. Same bottle for cooking — level tsp, not a free pour.
  • Toned paneer + toned dahi for cooking. Snack buttermilk: 200 ml from the 1 L tetra — plain salted only, no masala/pudina/chili. No coffee, alcohol, or soda. No tomato, raw onion, garlic, red chili, mint, lauki. KILRR OK on chicken — if reflux flares, switch to a milder pack.
  • After lunch/dinner: saunf or ajwain water. Sit upright 20-30 min. Elevate bed head if nights are rough.
  • Week 1 scale may rise 1–1.5 kg (glycogen water). Judge the 7-day average only.

Weekly split

Every day: 10,000 treadmill steps first, then lifting on gym days. Upright, incline OK. This is the fat-loss lever after the 1,500 kcal plate.

Training around GERD

  • Every session: 10k treadmill steps first, then weights. Stay upright.
  • Train at least 2 hours after eating, never right after a meal. Morning gym → cheela after. Evening gym → after lunch, dinner still 8:30.
  • Skip lying-flat crunches/sit-ups and heavy leg raises. Use standing cable crunches, planks, or Pallof presses instead.
  • Avoid deep forward folds or inversions on a full stomach.
  • Sip water through the session, don't gulp large amounts at once.
  • Protein ~150 g/day from the weekday plate so the 15 kg is mostly fat and you can still lift. Add-ons only if a meal is missed or the session was brutal.
