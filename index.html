How the week works

Home (morning, every day) = 30-min incline walk first (warm 3%/4.5 → fat-burn 12%/4.8 → cool 2%/4.0), then core + pull-up path. Wed/Sat add 3 band compounds. Gym (evening) Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri = 6 lifts only + optional easy walk after — no hard incline before bars. Wed/Sat/Sun = no gym lifts (incline already done at Home). Alternative = miss-gym makeup.

  • Morning cardio + evening lifts on purpose — keeps strength work fresh (less cardio interference).
  • Miss the gym? Open Alternative and run that block instead of the Gym lifts.
  • Train at least 2 hours after lunch. Never right after a meal. Dinner stays at 7:30 — stop eating 3 hrs before bed.
  • Lifts: do the exact sets and reps written for each move. Stop a set if form breaks. Do not grind to failure.
  • Progression: finish every listed rep with good form for 2 gym sessions in a row → next time add 2.5 kg on upper-body lifts or 5 kg on leg lifts (or keep the same weight and add 1 rep next block if plates are awkward). Every 5th week: cut each exercise to 2 sets and use a lighter weight.
  • Core stays lumbar-friendly: dead bug, glute bridge, side plank. Stop if lumbar pain — drop that move that day.
  • Inverted row builds toward pull-ups. Advance: hang → scapular pulls → inverted row easy at 3 × 8 → band-assisted pull-ups → slow negatives → strict pull-ups.
  • Treadmill is lock-in numbers — no ranges. Fat-burn block stays at 12% · 4.8 km/h for 20 min. Lumbar or reflux flare: drop main to 8% · 4.8 km/h that day, keep the same times.
  • Skip rope (Alternative): soft knees, easy pace. Flare → swap for an easy incline walk.
  • Avoid heavy barbell deadlifts, bent-over barbell rows, and good mornings while the back settles. DB Romanian hinge on Gym B is the posterior-chain lift (soft knees).
  • Avoid deep forward folds or inversions on a full stomach.
  • Sip water through the session; don't gulp large amounts at once.
  • Protein ~134 g/day weekdays. Miss a meal → whey protein on that meal’s Today card.
