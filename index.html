Reflux ground rules

  • Stop eating 3 hrs before lying down. Dinner is 8:30 PM — then a 10-min upright walk.
  • Weigh raw grams for chicken, rice, atta, paneer, oil (level tsp). Eyeballing breaks the deficit.
  • Olive oil hard cap: 3 level tsp/day. Light/refined for cooking; EVOO only on salads.
  • Toned paneer + toned dahi only. No coffee, alcohol, or soda. Sat muesli (40 g weighed) is the chocolate exception. KILRR packs (incl. pudina/peri/mirchi/achaari) OK on chicken — if reflux flares, switch to milder packs next day.
  • After lunch/dinner: saunf or ajwain water. Sit upright 20-30 min. Elevate bed head if nights are rough.
  • Week 1 scale may rise 1–1.5 kg (glycogen water). Judge the 7-day average only.

Weekly split

Walk 8,000-10,000 steps every day regardless of gym day. This is your biggest fat-loss lever after diet, and it is GERD-friendly (upright, low-impact).

Training around GERD

  • Train at least 2 hours after eating, never right after a meal.
  • Skip lying-flat crunches/sit-ups and heavy leg raises. Use standing cable crunches, planks, or Pallof presses instead.
  • Avoid deep forward folds or inversions on a full stomach.
  • Sip water through the session, don't gulp large amounts at once.
  • Protein target ~150 g/day keeps the 17 kg mostly fat. Add-ons only after a brutal session — base day is already 1,500.
