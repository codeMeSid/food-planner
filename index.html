Reflux ground rules

  • Stop eating 3 hrs before lying down. Bigger lever than any single food.
  • Smaller, more frequent meals beat 3 large ones.
  • Sit upright 20-30 min after eating. Elevate the head of your bed if nights are rough.
  • Coffee, alcohol, carbonated drinks, mint and chocolate are cut out entirely.

Weekly split

Walk 8,000-10,000 steps every day regardless of gym day. This is your biggest fat-loss lever after diet, and it is GERD-friendly (upright, low-impact).

Training around GERD

  • Train at least 2 hours after eating, never right after a meal.
  • Skip lying-flat crunches/sit-ups and heavy leg raises. Use standing cable crunches, planks, or Pallof presses instead.
  • Avoid deep forward folds or inversions on a full stomach.
  • Sip water through the session, don't gulp large amounts at once.
  • Protein target (~150g/day) matters as much as the workouts for keeping muscle while losing 17 kg. Don't skip meals around training.
