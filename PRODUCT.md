# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person following a GERD-safe Indian meal-prep plan while losing fat (95→80 kg by 1 Jan). Primary surface is a phone home-screen PWA: Mon–Fri same four meals with Sunday batch steps on tap, gym split (10k steps then lift), weekly grocery in shop packs. Used in the kitchen and at the gym, often one-handed.

## Product Purpose

Make the weekday plate (Sunday batch + day-of assembly), workout, food rules, and a Mon–Fri grocery list available offline with no account. Success is opening Today, cooking the weighed recipe, hitting ~1,500 kcal / ~150 g protein, and buying only what the week needs. Sat/Sun are free eating.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, kitchen-practical. Ceramic-sand page (`#E3CFBB`) with clay surfaces and terracotta clay accent from the brand 3-stripe palette. Voice is instructional, not motivational. Numbers and times do the talking. No fitness-app hype.

## Anti-references

- Cool-gray SaaS dashboards (ink stays warm)
- Calorie-counter chrome and gamified streaks
- SaaS metric heroes (giant number + gradient bar as the whole brand)
- Dark gym neon / forest-wellness dashboards with glow

## Design Principles

- The task is the UI: Today first, chrome second.
- One accent family (terracotta clay) for selection and primary action; deepen fills for AA; semantics (risk, danger) stay readable, not decorative.
- Familiar mobile patterns (bottom tabs, chips, native dialog). Invent nothing for flavor.
- Device-local honesty: saved-here copy, no fake sync.
- Offline is a requirement, not a bonus: self-hosted fonts in SW cache, no runtime font CDNs, relative asset URLs.
- Quiet chrome: surface color over borders, 4/8 spacing, restrained pills.
- Macros are computed from weighed ingredient lines — copy never invents a different number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on body, labels, placeholders, and selected tab states. Visible `:focus-visible` rings. `prefers-reduced-motion` kills motion. Touch targets ≥44px on tabs, chips, and meal rows. Recipe dialog: Escape, backdrop, close button; focus restore. GERD-related copy stays plain language.
